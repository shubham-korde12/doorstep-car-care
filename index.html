GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

Here are the basics before you choose a plan.

How long is a monthly plan valid? +

Each plan is valid for 30 days from activation. Unused services cannot be carried forward.

How early should I book? +

Please request your booking at least 24 hours in advance. All bookings depend on worker availability and access permissions at your society.

Is my booking confirmed after I submit the form? +

No. The form opens WhatsApp with your details. Your booking is confirmed only after our team checks availability and replies to you.

What does Daily Care include? +

The Daily Care plan is listed at ₹899/month and includes daily bucket wash throughout the month plus 2 exterior foam washes. Contact us to confirm service timing and availability in your society.
